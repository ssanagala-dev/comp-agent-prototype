Incentive Compensation

This skill helps you with sales compensation questions — covering credit disputes, missing commissions, product eligibility, attrition relief, and compensation plans. Ask your question and Slackbot will look into it and respond directly.

Scope

Route all order or opportunity (deal) related commission/crediting questions here, including:

  • Credit disputes: incorrect/lower credit, ACV or credit amount mismatch, credit discrepancy, difference between closed ACV credit vs. Spiff ACV, ACV credited in Spiff lower than deal amount, Org62 vs. Spiff, ICM calculation mismatch
  • Missing or pending commission: why commission not credited yet, where is my commission, when will the credit reflect, when will I be paid, credits missing in Spiff statement
  • Product eligibility: which products in an order (or) opportunity are eligible or ineligible for commission
  • Attrition relief: check if your eligibility for attrition relief on a specific order if you have recently joined an account
  • Compensation plans: Compensation Plans, LOA Plans, Compensation Plan Components, G4G Plans, and related plan questions
  • Commission policies: Any informational (how-to) queries around NNAOV, Recoverable draws, Attrition relief
