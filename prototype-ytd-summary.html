How the YTD Earnings Summary would work

This is a concept for the Comp skill forum, based on the working Claude skill from Srini's and Mahesh's team. Data pulls stay fixed and repeatable. The AI only handles the conversation.

1. Seller asksTaps the "YTD summary" button or asks in plain words ("what have I earned this year?").AI · intent
2. Who's askingThe seller comes from their Slack login. No employee ID box, so they can only ever pull their own data.Fixed rule
3. Pull the dataThe same queries every time: frozen Spiff statements, credits, attainment, clawbacks, draws (Snowflake through the Sales Agent MCP).No AI
4. Check the totalsThe totals are checked against the seller's statements before anything is shown. If they don't match, no doc is made and the seller is told why.No AI
5. Build the docA fixed Google Doc template is filled in and saved to the seller's Drive. Only the seller can see it until they share it.Template

What the seller gets

  • In Slack: a short snapshot (earned, clawbacks, net paid) and a choice of a Google Doc or just the answer in chat.
  • In the doc: what they see at a glance, quota and attainment by component, month by month, clawbacks with order numbers, draws, and a "matches your statements" check.
  • After: they can ask follow-ups about the doc in the same chat ("why was $8,000 clawed back?"), and those answers use the existing Spiff use cases (PPA, BCF).
